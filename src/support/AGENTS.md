# support

## Purpose

Human support: the support cases (incidents) opened by customers or by the agent's `request_human` tool, the operator's case list and dashboard workspace, the operator's case view that explains a case with its conversation, agent events, transfers and the bank's record, and the **verified case brief** (Part 2, commit 4): every claim the assistant made, judged by code against the bank, the app and the policy rules, with findings, procedures, a timeline and a template reply. The brief is deterministic for now (claims found by pattern matching; no model is called).

Hexagonal vertical (plan §10, 7d.3): `domain/` (pure rules), `application/` (ports, use cases, and the one service `case-facts`), `infrastructure/` (SQLite, telemetry and bank adapters), `entry-points/` (wiring, http, tools). No `legacy.ts`: no frozen path reaches this feature. Nothing else lives at the root but `index.ts` and this file.

## For AI Agents

### Working In This Directory

- `operatorId` must come from `actor()`. The operator is checked twice: on the session role in the handler, and on the person behind `actor.id` in `GetCaseDetail` and `case-facts` (`requireOperator`, before the case). The brief and reply handlers gate the role before they reads the body. Both gates, the case's customer, the bank call signed as the operator, the operation filter, the tool's check order, the case owner (`ctx.userId`) and the one-open-case dedupe are pinned by mutation.
- Rank 2: value-imports only `identity`, `conversations`, `knowledge` (rank 0) and `transfers` (rank 1), through their indexes and only in `entry-points/dependencies.ts`; `assistant` only as `import type { Tool }`.
- Lint (`eslint.config.mjs`): support is a layered vertical, so `index.ts` re-exports only `./entry-points/` (types allowed) and `src/server/` reaches it only through the index; `seed.ts` may also import its `infrastructure/`.
- Things worth scrutinizing:
  - `GetCaseDetail` reconciles through `transfers().getConversationTransfers({ userId: incident.user_id, … })`, which signs the bank call as the customer.
  - `incident` is returned whole; no scoping beyond the role check (every operator sees every case).
  - Part 2: the deterministic brief (commit 4) and "Send and close" (commit 5) have landed; the model-written brief (commit 6) and the eval (commit 7) follow. `replyAndCloseCase` is the only writer of `closed`, so `previousCases[].closedAt` is the real closing time. A customer who writes after the close gets a new case only through `request_human` (no automatic reopening). The reply is rechecked at send time against the bank, never trusted from the brief.
  - The brief's evidence: unmeasured or unavailable is never verified. The regex path is conservative by default: a pattern-matched amount or status outside the whitelisted clause shape is ⚪, never ✅/❌; nuance is left to the model extraction (commit 6), which still goes through the oracle. A bank that cannot be reached, or that refuses, is an explicit `bank_unreachable` (verdict and finding, each saying which), and a stored `failed`, `processing` or `unknown` intent settles nothing then. A negated status phrase is a ⚪ claim, never dropped and never read as its positive.
  - Policy values come only through `policyFor` / `productOfAccount`. No prompt exists yet, so no customer text is fenced (`fence` lands with the model brief).
